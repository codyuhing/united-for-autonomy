Who We Are

United for Autonomy is a coalition of forward-thinking industries and mission-driven nonprofits united to advance U.S. leadership in autonomous vehicle innovation. We are committed to ensuring this transformative technology delivers on its promise of safety, accessibility, economic growth, and supply chain resilience. Through collaboration, advocacy, and education, we work to shape policies and public understanding that secure America's position at the forefront of autonomous mobility for the benefit of all.
