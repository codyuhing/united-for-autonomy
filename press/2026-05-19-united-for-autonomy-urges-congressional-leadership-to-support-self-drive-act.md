WASHINGTON -- Members of United for Autonomy, a coalition dedicated to advancing a federal policy framework for autonomous vehicles (AVs) in the United States, sent a letter to House Energy and Commerce Committee Chairman Brett Guthrie and Ranking Member Frank Pallone and Subcommittee on Commerce, Manufacturing and Trade Chairman Gus Bilirakis and Ranking Member Jan Schakowsky urging support for the SELF DRIVE Act.

From the letter: "The SELF DRIVE Act makes strides toward enabling the safe and timely deployment of AV technology and solidifying the U.S. as the global leader in this transformative field. While AVs have been deployed in recent years, the United States lacks a federal policy framework to set uniform rules of the road for these vehicles."

Read the full letter [here](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/67ee2ad971d86c70d02ed03f/6a1dd4fe38d5341098065576_United%20for%20Autonomy%20Letter%20SELF%20DRIVE%202026.pdf).

The SELF DRIVE Act was introduced by Congressman Bob Latta (R-OH) and would help establish a federal policy framework for autonomous vehicles.
